# B3. Conduct Code and Conflict Ladder
**We agree to:**
- No violence, threats, or intimidation
- No theft or damage to shared property or crops
- No harassment, bullying, or sexual misconduct
- No abuse or neglect of children, elders, or animals
- Respect quiet hours, shared spaces, and others' belongings
- Respect everyone's beliefs, and not promote any belief in shared spaces
- Be honest with money, keys, and records
- Follow the group's written policies on substances and weapons *[attach, within local law]*, including the firearms policy ([Appendix B11](b11-firearms-policy-template.md))
- Follow fire and cooking safety rules, and never tamper with detectors, fire barriers, locks, cameras, or recording equipment

**Conflict ladder:** (1) talk directly, (2) ask a neutral member, (3) mediation, (4) board hearing (closed and unrecorded), (5) outside mediation or an attorney.

**Warnings and removal:** warning, written agreement to change, review, then removal. Decisions are made by the board in writing, with a right of appeal. Immediate safety problems go to the authorities at once. *(Attorney to confirm tenant and notice rules before any removal from housing.)*
