# B2. First-Meeting Agenda
1. Welcome, and why each person is here
2. Read and adjust the purpose and values
3. Walk through the honest conversation list ([Chapter 2](../../part-1-core-chapters/02-forming-a-group.md))
4. Name roles and backups
5. Agree on the decision method and meeting schedule
6. Assign contact with the accountant, the market manager, and a landowner or host
7. Set the next three actions, with names and dates
8. **Close:** who writes up the minutes and when
