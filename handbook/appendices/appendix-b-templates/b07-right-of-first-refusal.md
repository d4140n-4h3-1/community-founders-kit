# B7. Right of First Refusal *(attorney drafts; outline only)*
- The community has the first chance to buy if the owner decides to sell
- The owner must give written notice of any offer received, with its full terms
- The community has *[number]* days to match the offer
- The notice covers what is included (land, water, mineral rights)
- It applies to *[described land]* and lasts *[term]*
