# B11. Firearms Policy Template *(attorney reviews before use)*
1. **Purpose:** to keep everyone safe around firearms. These rules apply even where the law is less strict.
2. **The four rules:** treat every firearm as loaded, never point it at anything you are not willing to destroy, keep your finger off the trigger until ready to shoot, and know your target and what is beyond it.
3. **Semi-automatic rifles and shotguns:** locked in a bolted safe, unloaded, with ammunition stored separately, whenever not in hand. Kept in the owner's home only. Transported unloaded in a locked case. Not used for farm work or patrol.
4. **Pistols:** carried concealed only, in a holster that covers the trigger guard, with any permit the law requires, and never set down. Locked up when not carried. A handgun safety course before carrying on community land.
5. **Manual rifles and shotguns (farm tools):** for livestock protection and humane euthanasia only, by named, trained adults: *[names]*. Unloaded and locked when not in use, in *[safe or cabinet location]*. Loaded only at the place of use. Shot only with a safe backstop, following the shooting-zone map. Rifles never fired toward a road, building, ridge, or skyline. No one goes out alone at night with a firearm.
6. **Never allowed:** *[the Lodge, the Meeting Hall, the shared kitchen, the food store, the bathhouse, greenhouses, children's areas, governance meetings and hearings, the market stand unless market rules allow concealed pistols, anyone impaired, anyone the law prohibits]*
7. **Children:** no access to firearms, ammunition, keys, or combinations. Taught "Don't touch. Leave the area. Tell an adult."
8. **Visitors:** follow the same rules, are told before they arrive, and never use a farm gun.
9. **Crisis:** any member may ask for temporary off-site storage, and asking is never held against them. Brandishing or threatening with a firearm falls under the immediate safety exception.
10. **Records:** owners sign the yearly storage statement below. No list of personal firearms is kept. Community farm guns are listed, with two key custodians: *[name]* and *[name]*. Every shot fired on the land is logged ([Appendix D19](../appendix-d-logs-sheets-and-reports/d19-firearm-discharge-log.md)).
