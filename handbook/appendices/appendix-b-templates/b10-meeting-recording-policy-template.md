# B10. Meeting Recording Policy Template *(attorney reviews before use)*
1. **Purpose:** to keep a historical archive of open community and board meetings. Written minutes remain the official record.
2. **Place and equipment:** the Meeting Hall only, with one dedicated camera and microphone, an indicator light that is on only while recording, and a black-and-yellow "RECORDING IN PROGRESS" sign at the entrance.
3. **What is recorded:** open meetings, votes, budget and tier reviews, and public hearings.
4. **Never recorded:** conflict-ladder, removal, and appeal hearings, safeguarding matters, personal, health, or family matters, complaints about named individuals, legal advice, and anything involving a minor's private information. Closed sessions are announced, the recording is paused, and the minutes note the pause and its legal basis.
5. **Audio:** recorded only after an attorney confirms local consent rules and the board approves in writing.
6. **Consent and notice:** members consent in the membership agreement. Every meeting opens with the recording announcement. Visitors are told at the door and may use the unrecorded seat, give written comment, or leave. Minors are not recorded.
7. **Archivists:** *[name]* and *[name]*, who hold the equipment cabinet keys and the storage access.
8. **Storage:** off-site or cloud backup and a second offline copy, kept separate from security-camera footage.
9. **Retention:** open-meeting recordings are kept permanently as the archive unless the board votes otherwise under a written schedule: *[schedule]*.
10. **Access:** residents may view the archive on request, with a log entry. Public release of any excerpt needs board approval and redaction of names and private details.
11. **No editing:** originals are never altered or deleted except under the retention schedule. Excerpts are labeled.
12. **Objections:** a person may ask for a correction note or review, handled by the board under the conflict ladder.
13. **Inspection and checklist:** monthly with Appendix A19, and every meeting with Appendix A20.
