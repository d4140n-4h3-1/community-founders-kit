# B5. Residents' Agreement *(attorney drafts)*
Key clauses to include:
1. Parties and the specific site or lot
2. Term, notice to end, and renewal
3. **Fee:** amount, due date, what it covers, how and when it may change, and notice required
4. Payment method, late-payment steps (reminder, payment plan, board review), and the **hardship or work-trade option**
5. Rules: conduct code, quiet hours, visitors, pets, structures, safety equipment, not tampering with detectors, fire barriers, locks, or cameras, and the occupancy limit of the cabin
6. Maintenance duties for the resident and the community
7. Entry and inspection rights, with notice
8. Insurance and liability
9. Ending the agreement: the fair removal process, appeal, belongings, deposits
10. Dispute resolution
11. Equal treatment, privacy (no cameras at homes), and local housing law compliance

**Signed:** *[resident / community representative / date]*
