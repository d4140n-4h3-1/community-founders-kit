# B9. Cameras and Surveillance Policy Template *(attorney reviews before use)*
1. **Purpose:** to deter vandalism and theft and to record incidents in shared and outdoor areas. Not to monitor residents.
2. **Locations:** *[list: food store door, main gate, greenhouse door, pump house, loading area]*. Never bathrooms, cabin interiors, windows into homes, sleeping or changing areas, the Lodge, or children's play areas.
3. **Audio:** none, unless an attorney confirms it is lawful and the board approves it in writing. The Meeting Hall recording is the one named exception (B10).
4. **Visible standard:** bright orange housings, real working units, mounted high with cables in conduit. A neutral backup camera is disclosed to the board and described here: *[location]*.
5. **Signs:** black with yellow stenciled letters reading "VIDEO RECORDING IN USE" and "PRIVATE PROPERTY," at the entrance to each camera area.
6. **Storage:** off-site or cloud, with battery or solar backup and an offline alert. Never shared with Meeting Hall storage.
7. **Custodians:** *[name]* and *[name]*. Every viewing is logged in the camera access log.
8. **Who reviews:** the safeguarding lead and the board, with a second person present, only for incidents.
9. **Retention:** *[30]* days, then deletion, unless an incident needs it kept longer.
10. **Sharing:** with police or insurers only through the proper process, with board approval and a log entry. Never posted online. Footage of children is never shared publicly.
11. **Resident rights:** residents may ask what is recorded and where, and raise concerns through the conflict ladder.
12. **Inspection:** monthly, using Appendix A19.
