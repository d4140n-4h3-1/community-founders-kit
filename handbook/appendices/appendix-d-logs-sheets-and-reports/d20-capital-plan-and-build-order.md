# D20. Capital Plan and Build Order *(draft at Tier 1, use through Tier 3)*

Tier 2 is where the community starts building, and it costs far more than a market stand earns in a season. Draft this plan during Tier 1, so the money is found before the work starts. The Tier 1 → Tier 2 trigger asks for it.

**Plan rules**

- Build in this order unless a permit or an inspector requires otherwise. Each step either earns income or is needed by the next one.
- Get two quotes for any item above the charter's spending limit, and add 10% to every estimate for surprises.
- **Count a grant or donation only once it is awarded in writing.** Seek them early, but never start work on a promise.
- **No item starts until its money is in hand.** The operating reserve is never used for building ([Chapter 8](../../part-2-market-engine/08-money-rules/02-reserve-and-inflation-protection.md)).
- Review the plan at the monthly governance meeting, and record changes in the revision log.

**Where the money can come from**

- Market surplus after the reserve contribution
- Farm, food, and community grants (ask the extension service, a community foundation, and local farm organizations which ones fit)
- Donations of money, and in-kind gifts such as surplus block, used steel buildings, gravel, or drywall
- Volunteer build days with a safety briefing and a first-aider on site
- Seller or partner support written into the land-use agreement, such as the owner paying for a well

*If the plan cannot be funded, the community stays at Tier 1 a while longer. That is the plan working, not failing.*
