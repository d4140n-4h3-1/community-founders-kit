# D21. Labor Budget *(weekly, for the busiest weeks of the season)*

## Closing a Gap

Try these in order, and record the choice in the minutes:

1. Plant fewer beds or fewer crops, and keep the ones that earn the most per hour ([D4](d04-yield-and-cost-sheet.md))
2. Simplify the work: drip irrigation on timers, mulch, fewer shared meals a week
3. Hold a volunteer day for one-off jobs
4. Move a project to the off-season
5. Recruit, at the pace the group can absorb ([Chapter 5](../../part-1-core-chapters/05-people-and-safeguarding/01-membership-and-conduct.md))

Never close the gap by asking the same few people to work more, or by tying hours to anyone's housing.
