# A2. Weekly Sanitation
- [ ] Toilets serviced and stocked (soap, paper, sanitizer)
- [ ] Handwashing stations filled, soap available
- [ ] Drinking water containers cleaned on schedule
- [ ] Trash and compost handled, with no standing food waste
- [ ] Kitchen and wash-and-pack surfaces cleaned
- [ ] Signs for handwashing visible
- [ ] Pests checked (droppings, gaps, standing water)
