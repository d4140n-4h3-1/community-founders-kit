# A20. Meeting Recording Checklist *(every recorded meeting)*
**Before**
- [ ] Meeting posted with the date, agenda, and notice that it will be recorded
- [ ] Entrance sign and indicator light working, and an unrecorded seating area ready
- [ ] Secretary and backup named for the written minutes
- [ ] No minors in view of the camera, or the meeting held without them
- [ ] Equipment unlocked by an archivist, and a test recording checked

**During**
- [ ] Chair announces at the start: "This meeting is being recorded for the community archive."
- [ ] Visitors reminded they may sit in the unrecorded area or give written comment
- [ ] Closed sessions announced, the recording paused, and the minutes note the pause and the legal basis (not the content)
- [ ] Recording restarted after the closed session, with the time noted
- [ ] Votes stated clearly and written in the minutes

**After**
- [ ] Recording stopped, and the indicator light off
- [ ] File named by date, saved to the off-site backup and the offline copy
- [ ] Index entry added ([Appendix D18](../appendix-d-logs-sheets-and-reports/d18-meeting-archive-index.md))
- [ ] Minutes approved and filed as the official record
- [ ] Equipment locked in the cabinet, and keys signed in
- [ ] Any request to correct or review a segment logged for the board
