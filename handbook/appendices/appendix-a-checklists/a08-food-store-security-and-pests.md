# A8. Food-Store Security and Pests *(monthly)*
- [ ] Door, hinges, lock, and strike plate intact
- [ ] Roof, vents, and windows secure, with no gaps
- [ ] Key list and sign-out log reviewed
- [ ] Shelves off the floor, and stock rotated first in, first out
- [ ] Cold storage temperature logged
- [ ] Traps checked, and no droppings or damage
- [ ] Floor drain clear, and no damp or mold
- [ ] Emergency light, smoke detector, and extinguisher tested
- [ ] No garden chemicals stored here
