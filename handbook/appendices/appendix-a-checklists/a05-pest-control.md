# A5. Pest Control
- [ ] Screens and door seals intact
- [ ] No standing water within reach of cabins
- [ ] Food and compost sealed or covered
- [ ] Porch lights are amber bug lights, aimed down and shielded
- [ ] Garden checked weekly, and any spray logged (date, product, place, why)
- [ ] No broad-spectrum pesticides in or near gardens
- [ ] Garden spiders left in place, and dangerous species identified and kept out of living areas (Chapter 15)
