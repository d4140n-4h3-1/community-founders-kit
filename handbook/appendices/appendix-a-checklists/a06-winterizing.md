# A6. Winterizing *(where freezing occurs)*
- [ ] Hoses drained and disconnected, outdoor lines drained
- [ ] Exposed pipes and pump housing insulated
- [ ] Main shutoff location posted in each building
- [ ] Heaters and stoves inspected, clearances checked
- [ ] Smoke and CO detectors tested, batteries replaced
- [ ] Cold-snap plan reviewed: blankets, sleeping bags, warm shared room, stored water
- [ ] Hoop house and greenhouse covers checked, frost cloth ready, and the temperature alarm tested
- [ ] Roads and ditches cleared
