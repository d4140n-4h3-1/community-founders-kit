# A18. Greenhouse Security and Crop Protection *(monthly, with the daily check logged on the chore chart)*
**Security**
- [ ] Inside lock opens with one hand and no key, and the exit is unblocked
- [ ] Outside keyed lock works, and the key log is current with two key holders
- [ ] Check that the greenhouse is empty before locking, and a second person confirms at dusk
- [ ] Hinges, door, and frame sound, and hinges cannot be lifted off
- [ ] Glazing (polycarbonate panels or film) intact, with no cuts or cracks
- [ ] Vent mesh or bars secure, with airflow still working
- [ ] Base, buried edge, or low wall intact, with nothing for climbing against the roof edge
- [ ] Motion light works, and the camera and sign are in place
- [ ] No tools, chemicals, fuel, cash, or card reader stored inside

**Crop protection**
- [ ] Temperature alarm tested (high and low) and sending alerts to two people
- [ ] Battery or solar vent opener tested, and manual vent opening practiced
- [ ] Stored water reserve full, and the pump house, shutoffs, and breaker box locked
- [ ] Tray counts and dated photos taken
- [ ] Footbath or boot change and clean tools in use
- [ ] Heaters, grow lights, and timers checked, and fire rules followed
- [ ] Wind anchors and covers checked
- [ ] Losses, damage, and causes logged (Appendix D13 or D16)
