## LED Buying Guidelines

Check each item before buying any light, including donated lights and holiday strings.

- [ ] Energy-efficiency label (ENERGY STAR, DLC, or your region's equivalent)
- [ ] Warm white (2700-3000K) for living spaces, neutral white (3500-4000K) for work areas
- [ ] Enclosed-rated LEDs in sealed fixtures
- [ ] Dimmable LEDs only with compatible dimmers
- [ ] Outdoor-rated, sealed fixtures for roads, entrances, and greenhouses
- [ ] Amber bug-light LEDs for porches ([Chapter 15](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/15-porch-lights-and-garden-spiders.md))
- [ ] Outdoor lights aimed down and shielded, on timers or motion sensors
- [ ] Bought in bulk once fixture types are known

## Solar Tube Choosing and Installing

**Building:** *[ ]* **Room size:** *[ ]* **Installer:** *[ ]*

- [ ] Sized to the room: roughly one 10-14 inch (25-35 cm) tube per 100-150 square feet (10-14 m²)
- [ ] Sealed dome, insulated tube, and gasketed diffuser
- [ ] Low-heat or insulated dome where summers are hot
- [ ] Flashing kit made for metal roofing, with every seam sealed
- [ ] Tube short and straight, with as few elbows as possible
- [ ] Wind and hail ratings confirmed with the manufacturer
- [ ] Tube insulated through the attic
- [ ] Night-light option considered (an LED fixture in the same opening)
- [ ] Tube kept out of fire-rated walls and ceilings, or a rated collar or housing used ([Chapter 9](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/09-shelter-evolution/04-interior-fire-protection.md))
- [ ] Not counted as an egress window
