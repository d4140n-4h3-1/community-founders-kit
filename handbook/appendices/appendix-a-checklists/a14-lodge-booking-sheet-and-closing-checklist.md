**Closing checklist**
- [ ] Occupancy limit not exceeded
- [ ] Tables and chairs reset, floor swept
- [ ] Kitchen cleaned, food removed or stored sealed
- [ ] Trash and compost taken out
- [ ] Group's own items taken home, with nothing left on the walls
- [ ] Heater or mini-split set back, lights off, windows and doors locked
- [ ] Key returned and signed in
