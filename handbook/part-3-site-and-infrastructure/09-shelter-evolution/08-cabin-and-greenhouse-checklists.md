# Cabin and Greenhouse Checklists

## Cabin Checklist (steel or block)
**Structure and legal**
- [ ] **Shell is noncombustible:** steel at minimum, or filled block
- [ ] Foundation: gravel pad, piers, or slab, depending on local code (sized for the number of stories and the shell material)
- [ ] Two stories maximum, counting any loft used for sleeping
- [ ] Cabin rated for no more than four residents, with a separate egress and detector for each bedroom
- [ ] Upper floors tied to the foundation and anchored against wind ([Chapter 16](../16-wind-power-and-wind-safety.md))
- [ ] Written approval or permit for the use (storage vs. living)

**Safety**
- [ ] Every sleeping level has its own egress window or exit
- [ ] Stairs have handrails and are lit
- [ ] Smoke and carbon monoxide detectors (tested monthly) and a fire extinguisher
- [ ] Heater vented outside, with manufacturer clearances
- [ ] **No unvented combustion heaters indoors**
- [ ] **Interior drywall or approved fire-rated finish installed,** with penetrations sealed and joints finished
- [ ] Foam insulation covered by a thermal barrier

**Summer cooling**
- [ ] Shaded, vented roof over the attic
- [ ] White or reflective roof coating
- [ ] Ridge, gable, or soffit vents (ember-resistant where wildfire is a risk)
- [ ] Windows on opposite walls for cross-breezes
- [ ] Ceiling or box fans
- [ ] Mini-split when power allows

**Winter warmth**
- [ ] Insulated roof, walls, and floor, with a vapor barrier
- [ ] Doors, windows, and floor edges air-sealed
- [ ] Main windows face the sun (south in the northern hemisphere, north in the southern), with an overhang that blocks summer sun but admits winter sun
- [ ] Heavy curtains or window inserts at night

**Lighting and pests**
- [ ] LED fixtures only
- [ ] Solar tube installed with a metal-roof flashing kit and an insulated tube, using a rated collar where it passes through a rated ceiling (see [Chapter 14](../14-lighting-standard.md))
- [ ] Amber bug-light LED on every porch
- [ ] Screens on windows and doors, with tight seals

## Greenhouse Checklist
- [ ] Full sun, near water, shielded from wind
- [ ] Anchored well, since wind lifts light structures
- [ ] Roof or side vents to prevent overheating, with metal mesh or bars so nothing can be reached through them
- [ ] LED grow lights only, if used
- [ ] Used for plants only, not sleeping
- [ ] **Door locks from the inside with a no-key exit, plus an outside keyed lock for when it is empty, with keys on the key log. No one is ever locked in.**
- [ ] Temperature alarm, backup vent opener, and stored water reserve ([Chapter 7, Crop Protection](../../part-2-market-engine/07-production-planning/04-crop-protection.md))
