# Membership and Conduct

## Principle
**Most communities fail over people, not infrastructure.** Clear membership rules, a fair conflict process, and real protection for children and vulnerable adults come before the first move-in. Write the rules while everyone still likes each other, apply them the same way to everyone, and keep the dignity of every person in the process. ([Chapter 2](../02-forming-a-group.md) covers how the founding group sets these up.)

## Who Can Join
- [ ] A written **membership agreement** every adult signs: shared rules, duties, how decisions are made, and consent to recording of open meetings once that policy is adopted ([Appendix B4](../../appendices/appendix-b-templates/b04-membership-agreement.md))
- [ ] A **trial period** (for example, 60-90 days) before full membership, for newcomers and the community alike
- [ ] Membership decisions by the group or board under written criteria, never by one person
- [ ] **Equal treatment:** no discrimination on protected grounds, **no religious or political tests,** and the same terms for everyone (check local fair-housing and anti-discrimination law, Chapters [3](../03-charter-and-decision-making.md) and [4](../04-legal-basics.md))
- [ ] Be honest about what the community offers and expects. Overpromising causes the worst departures.
- [ ] Set a **planned maximum size per tier,** counting people, with a family cabin counted at up to four, so growth never outruns water, septic, and leadership
- [ ] Keep a short **waiting list**, so new people join at a pace the group can absorb
- [ ] No one is asked to hand over savings, identity documents, or property as the price of joining

## Core Conduct Rules (put these in the charter)
- No violence, threats, or intimidation
- No theft, and no damage to shared property or crops
- No harassment, bullying, or sexual misconduct
- No abuse or neglect of children, elders, or animals
- Respect for quiet hours, shared spaces, and other people's belongings
- Respect for everyone's beliefs, with no pressure or promotion in shared spaces ([Chapter 3](../03-charter-and-decision-making.md))
- Honesty with money, keys, and records (Chapters [6](../../part-2-market-engine/06-market-business-basics/README.md) and [8](../../part-2-market-engine/08-money-rules/README.md))
- **Substances:** a written policy that follows local law. At minimum: no illegal drug dealing, no use by anyone handling food or machinery, no use by anyone responsible for children, and no impaired driving.
- **Weapons:** a written policy that follows local law, covering carrying, storage, and where they are never allowed. Decide it as a group before it becomes an argument. For firearms, start from the Firearms Safety section, which applies even where the law is less strict.
- **Fire and cooking:** open flames, stoves, and heaters follow the safety rules in Chapters [9](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/09-shelter-evolution/README.md) and [10](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/10-water-plumbing-and-septic/README.md), and no one tampers with detectors, fire barriers, locks, cameras, or recording equipment

## Visitors and the Public
- Visitors sign in, are hosted by a member, and follow the same conduct rules
- Set visitor hours and a limit on overnight stays
- Public areas (market stand, open day) stay separate from living areas
- Any member may ask a visitor to leave, and the host is responsible for them
- Keep a list of people who are not welcome, with the reason and date, reviewed by the board
