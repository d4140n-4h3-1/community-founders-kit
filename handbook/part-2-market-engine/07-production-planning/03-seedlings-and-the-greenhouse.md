# Seedlings and the Greenhouse

Seedlings are a strong early product, because spring buyers want plants before produce is ready.

- **Tier 1:** start in the hoop house, using clean trays and fresh potting mix
- **Tier 2:** move to a proper greenhouse, with benches, ventilation, LED grow lights on timers ([Chapter 14](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/14-lighting-standard.md)), and the security measures in [Chapter 9](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/09-shelter-evolution/README.md)
- **Tier 3:** a sand bed under the floor may extend the season ([Chapter 11](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/11-sand-battery-heating.md))
- Count back from the main spring planting date to set sowing dates, and don't start too early
- Label every tray with the variety and date, and keep a tray count so loss is noticed
- Harden plants off outdoors before sale
- Check plant-sale and nursery rules before selling ([Chapter 4](../../part-1-core-chapters/04-legal-basics.md))
- Sow modestly, and add more only after the first batch sells
