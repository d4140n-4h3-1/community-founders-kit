# Tier 3: Expand
- Two-story cabins (with sign-off), more greenhouses, additional market days
- Block cabins become a practical option for new housing, and every cabin keeps the noncombustible shell and fire-rated interior
- The Community Lodge built (or a permitted shared hall in use), with an equal-booking policy, a monthly cap per group, and no cameras
- **The Meeting Hall built (or a permitted hall designated), with an equal-booking policy, a dedicated camera and microphone under the recording policy, an indicator light and sign, two archivists, and off-site backup of the archive**
- Cold storage and a pack and loading area beside the food store
- Heated greenhouse for year-round production; yield, cost, and labor tracked per crop; security, alarms, and crop protection backups in every greenhouse, and crop and structure insurance reviewed
- Shared bathhouse or plumbed cabins; utility trench to the cabin cluster
- Culverts, turnarounds, and a loading apron
- Solar tubes in every cabin, the Lodge, the Meeting Hall, and the bathhouse
- Pilot sand bed in a greenhouse
- Optional pilot wind turbine for a shared building, greenhouse, or heat store, only with a year of wind data, engineer sign-off, and permits
- Motion-sensor LEDs on paths, roads, the pump house, and greenhouse doors; spider ID chart in common spaces
- More cameras (pump house, loading area, and a neutral backup camera) with off-site storage, an offline alert, a logged-access custodian pair, and a retention limit
- A board with non-residents hearing appeals, an independent route for complaints, and a leadership succession plan
- A bathhouse rota, a laundry schedule, volunteer days, and a monthly chore chart review
- Animals are optional: only if the group votes to keep them, with a care lead, backup, daily rota, and budget in place first
- A yearly budget by project, a capital plan for buildings and systems, and audited or independently reviewed records
- Reserve policy written into the charter, and an insured reserve account opened
- Form a nonprofit or land trust; apply for any tax exemption and document the basis for it; apply for grants; keep a land wish list and seek a right of first refusal
- Fee policy written into the charter (per planned lot, residents only), with an accountant's comparison of the exempt and non-exempt paths and an attorney's review, before buying land
