# Tier 3 → Tier 4 (Own)
- [ ] 2 full seasons of audited or independently reviewed records
- [ ] Nonprofit or land trust formed, with a board that includes non-residents
- [ ] Any claimed tax exemption granted in writing and documented
- [ ] Two annual reports published and all tax filings current
- [ ] Reserve fund covers 6 months of costs
- [ ] Reserve policy written into the charter and reviewed by an attorney or accountant
- [ ] Fee policy written into the charter (per planned lot, residents only), with the accountant's comparison of the exempt and non-exempt paths and the attorney's review on file
- [ ] Land purchase passes the due diligence and capacity checklists ([Chapter 13](../../part-3-site-and-infrastructure/13-land-acquisition-and-expansion.md)), including the property tax and deed-restriction items
- [ ] Legal review of the purchase completed by an attorney
- [ ] Succession plan so leadership can pass to new members
- [ ] Independent complaint route and appeal hearing in place
- [ ] Lodge built (or a permitted shared hall in use), with bookings run on equal terms and no cameras
- [ ] **Meeting Hall built, with the recording system, indicator, and sign working, two archivists named, off-site backup tested, and the archive index and access log current**
- [ ] Interior fire barriers inspected and logged for every cabin
- [ ] Camera and sign inspections, access logs, and the outside incident log reviewed by the board each year
- [ ] Greenhouse security and crop protection checklists completed monthly, with crop losses and causes reviewed
- [ ] Culverts and turnarounds in place; annual road maintenance logged
- [ ] Solar and wind production records reviewed before committing to a sand battery or added turbine
