# Tier 2 → Tier 3 (Expand)
- [ ] Reserve fund covers 2 months of operating costs, held in cash or insured accounts
- [ ] Spending limits written into the charter, and the first annual budget reviewed by the accountant
- [ ] Septic installed and inspected; wash-and-pack station passes any required inspection
- [ ] Block food store built, with key log and monthly pest inspections on record
- [ ] Greenhouse locks (inside no-key exit and outside keyed lock), vent mesh, key log, temperature alarm, and backup vent opener in place, and the locked pump house checked monthly
- [ ] Camera policy adopted and reviewed by an attorney, with orange cameras and black-and-yellow signs installed, off-site storage working, two custodians named, and the access log and retention limit in use
- [ ] **Meeting recording policy reviewed by an attorney, with recording consent in the membership agreement, and written minutes kept for every meeting**
- [ ] Wash-and-pack workflow and post-harvest cooling in regular use, with a harvest log
- [ ] At least 2 products selling consistently
- [ ] Two members trained to handle cash and bookkeeping
- [ ] Removal and appeal process reviewed by an attorney
- [ ] Joining and leaving process written down and reviewed by an attorney, and the first season review held
- [ ] Equal-treatment wording re-checked by an attorney before the Lodge and Meeting Hall open
- [ ] Shared kitchen rules, pet policy, and meal-cost method written down, and the first open day held
- [ ] First annual report published, with tax and fee payments shown
- [ ] Housing meets local code or has written approval for its current use
- [ ] Every occupied cabin meets local occupancy and egress rules, and none houses more than four residents
- [ ] Every cabin has the noncombustible shell, drywall or an approved fire-rated interior, and sealed penetrations
- [ ] Group agrees on a growth plan and budget in writing
- [ ] All roads to cabins, wash-and-pack, food store, and gardens graveled
- [ ] Emergency access reviewed with the local fire department
- [ ] 100% of fixtures are LED, with power use logged
- [ ] Wash-and-pack station and kitchen lit by solar tubes plus LED
- [ ] Every porch has a bug light, screens, and sealed doors
- [ ] Lodge and Meeting Hall permits, budgets, and septic readiness confirmed, with equal-booking policies written down
- [ ] *(Only if the group plans to keep animals:)* a named animal-care lead and backup, a written care sheet, a two-person daily rota, and feed, shelter, and vet costs in the budget, ready before any animals arrive
