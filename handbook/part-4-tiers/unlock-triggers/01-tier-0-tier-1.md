# Tier 0 → Tier 1 (Pilot)
- [ ] Written land permission in hand
- [ ] Local legal requirements checked and any needed approvals in writing ([Chapter 4](../../part-1-core-chapters/04-legal-basics.md))
- [ ] Founding agreement signed and reviewed by an attorney (including the equal-treatment wording), a two-signer community account open, roles named with backups, and a regular meeting schedule with written minutes ([Chapter 2](../../part-1-core-chapters/02-forming-a-group.md))
- [ ] Charter adopted by all members (includes the equal-treatment, LED, garden-spider, pesticide, food-storage, greenhouse-lock, camera-policy, meeting-recording, firearms, noncombustible-shell, cash-count, spending-limit, conduct and removal, outside-harassment response, hours guideline, cabin-occupancy, reserve, tax and reporting, resident-fee, and land-title rules, and what happens to money and property if the group dissolves)
- [ ] Membership agreement signed by all adults, with a first-aid kit, one trained first-aider, and posted emergency numbers
- [ ] The 12-hour guideline, a chore chart, an essential-task rota, shared-space rules, quiet hours, and a weekly rest day in use
- [ ] Accountant consulted, and a compliance calendar started with two responsible members
- [ ] A one-page budget and expense log in use, with receipts kept
- [ ] One full growing season of records, including a soil test, a crop plan, and a harvest log
- [ ] Safe drinking water and legal toilet access in place
- [ ] Boundary signs posted, a visitor sign-in in use, a named spokesperson, and the outside incident log started
- [ ] Market vendor permit and local food-sale rules confirmed, liability insurance in place, and sales tax registered if it applies, all before the first market day
- [ ] At least 3 market days attended with sales logged
- [ ] Market-day checklist in use, with two-person cash counts
- [ ] Core group of 5+ members still active
