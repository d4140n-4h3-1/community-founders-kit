**Example entry**

| Date | Tier | Type | What changed or was learned | Vote | Evidence | Recorded by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *2027-03-14* | *0 → 1* | *Tier vote* | *All Tier 0 → Tier 1 triggers met. Three market days were not enough to price well; we will log six before Tier 2.* | *7 / 0 / 1* | *Minutes 2027-03-14; tier review worksheet* | *[initials]* |

**When to write an entry**

- Every tier vote, whether the group advances, stays, or steps back ([Appendix E](handbook/appendices/appendix-e-tier-review-worksheet.md))
- Every change to the charter or a standing rule
- Any unlock-trigger threshold the group found too easy or too strict
- Every land decision, with the vote and the reasons ([Chapter 13](handbook/part-3-site-and-infrastructure/13-land-acquisition-and-expansion.md))
- The end-of-season review

> At graduation, share the lessons from this log with the handbook's maintainers (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md)), so the next groups benefit.
