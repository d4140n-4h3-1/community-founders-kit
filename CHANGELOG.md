# Changelog

Changes to the handbook itself, newest first. A community's own decisions go in its [revision log](REVISION_LOG.md).

## Unreleased

### Bonus: digital infrastructure

- New bonus page: community servers, networks, and public computers run Linux or one of the BSDs, while residents' private devices are not covered
- Fiber optic cable links the community's buildings wherever possible, for fast local file access, with no expectation of joining an outside fiber network; utility trenches lay conduit for it as they are dug
- FreeBSD is the default operating system for community systems and public computers; Linux or another BSD only where FreeBSD can't do a particular job, recorded on the network map
- ARM is the default hardware for infrastructure, to save energy; RISC-V only for light, non-critical services until FreeBSD supports it fully; x86 only where ARM can't do the job or for donated machines until they wear out
- Linux Mint Debian Edition (LMDE) is the default for public and office computers, on low-power x86 mini PCs, since LMDE runs only on x86; FreeBSD stays the default for infrastructure

### Better prepared founders (from a simulated founding group)

- Money rules judge a full year against the budget, so a planned off-season no longer forces a step back; the Tier 1 → Tier 2 income test covers a full season and off-season
- Before Tier 3, the governance meeting acts as the board, with a named outside advisor who joins reserve decisions and hears appeals
- Before anything permanent is built, the land-use agreement must run at least 10 years, set out who owns improvements, and include a right of first refusal or purchase option
- New Appendix D20 (capital plan and build order) and D21 (labor budget, with a worked example), both required by the Tier 1 → Tier 2 trigger
- Start Here lists what Tier 0 costs and lower-cost ways to pay for it, and how to plan for the off-season
- The Tier 1 → Tier 2 trigger no longer requires a conflict to have happened; a practice run of the ladder counts
- No one is ever locked in a hoop house: this rule is now in the core charter, since the Tier 1 hoop house has an outside hasp
- Default planned sizes per tier, recording and camera clauses in the membership agreement marked for when they apply, and smaller wording fixes

### Earlier changes

- The charter now grows by tier: Tier 0 → Tier 1 asks only for the core charter, and building, camera, recording, fee, and land rules are adopted at the tier that first needs them ([Chapter 3](handbook/part-1-core-chapters/03-charter-and-decision-making.md#when-each-rule-is-adopted))
- Appendices A3, A4, and A7 now hold full printable checklists instead of pointers
- Printable logs and sheets have full sets of blank rows; the local calendar lists all twelve months; the disaster plan lists the common hazards
- Worked examples added to the monthly budget (D5) and the fee worksheet (D11)
- Added Appendix F: Glossary, a community revision log template, this changelog, and a contributing guide
- Chapter 4 explains how to use the handbook outside the United States
- Every page ends with Previous and Next links in reading order
- Added automatic link, formatting, and navigation checks, and a script to build printable PDFs
